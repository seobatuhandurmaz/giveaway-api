Frequently Asked Questions About Instagram Giveaways

1. What is an Instagram Giveaway Picker?

An Instagram Giveaway Picker randomly selects winners from post comments, perfect for fair and transparent giveaways.

2. How do I run a giveaway on Instagram?

Create a post with clear rules, ask users to comment, then paste your URL into our picker and choose random winners.

3. Is this Instagram Giveaway Picker free?

Yes. You can use our tool for free for up to 500 comments, no login or signup required.

4. How does the giveaway picker work?

The picker uses API powered comment data and selects random winners instantly and securely.

5. Can I use the giveaway picker without login?

Yes. Just paste a public Instagram post link. You never need to log in or share credentials.

6. How to pick multiple winners on Instagram?

Enter the number of comments you want in the "Number of Comments" field, and the picker will draw that many winners.

7. What are Instagram giveaway rules?

You must include a disclaimer that Instagram is not affiliated with your contest and clearly define participation rules.

8. Does the picker support mobile?

Yes, our giveaway picker works perfectly on mobile devices and tablets.

9. What is the difference between a picker and a generator?

A picker randomly chooses from existing entries, while a generator simulates the randomization wheel visually.

10. How can I increase engagement during giveaways?

Ask users to tag friends, share your post, and follow your account to maximize visibility and participation.
